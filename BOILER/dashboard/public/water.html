Home water-consumption metering

Whole-house cold-water consumption in real liters, from a non-invasive clamp-on ultrasonic flow meter on the incoming cold main → an ESP32 → MQTT → this page, reconciled against the water bill. Status: PLANNED — meter on order (ships Sept 8–17). Nothing live yet.

Plan

  1. Meter: TUF-2000M clamp-on ultrasonic + TS-2 transducer (DN15–100, standard temp — cold water).
  2. ⚠ Mount on the BRASS section of the ¾″ cold main, not the PEX (PEX is too soft/lossy for ultrasonic). Straight run + full pipe.
  3. ESP32 + RS-485 (MAX485) reads the meter over Modbus (or counts its pulse output) → MQTT, via the esp_boards framework (like the Electra A/C board).
  4. This page: live flow + daily/monthly liters + trend.
  5. Reconcile metered usage against the water bill on the Boiler → Water tab.
