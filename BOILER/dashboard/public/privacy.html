Appointment colors

The next-appointment card is colored by how close it is. A far-off appointment is green; as it approaches it turns yellow then red. After the appointment day it stays red for the grey-days below, then turns grey forever. Green (far) is automatic.

🔴 Red — the days right before the appointment 🟡 Yellow — the days before the red band ⚪ Grey — days it stays red after the appointment, then grey
