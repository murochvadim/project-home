👟 Step counting

Walking trips from Project General → Recent trips are auto-converted to steps (per person, by phone) by a watcher on LXC 104. It reads each trip's actual GPS points and decides walk / drive / phantom from the point-to-point speeds — a drive (or a drive→park→drive) and GPS-ghost trips are skipped; steps come from the clean (good-accuracy) walked distance.

Defaults: 1300 steps/km · accuracy gate 35 m · phantom floor 150 m · driving 15 km/h · cap 30 km. Trips with no stored GPS points (aged out) are skipped, not guessed. Manual entries + trip-steps both add to the daily total.

💧 Water reminders

Spreads a daily cup target across your waking window and nudges you on the reminders badge every interval until you catch up. Each person logs their own cups on Personal Health → 💧 Water. Phone push (ntfy) comes later — for now the nudge shows on the dashboard badge.
